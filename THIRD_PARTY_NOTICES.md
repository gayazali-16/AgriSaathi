## Redistributed dataset and source documentation

The bounded historical extracts come from the combined agricultural dataset
documented in [the original README](docs/sources/crop-dataset-README.md),
[reference list](docs/sources/crop-dataset-references.pdf) and
[Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21981601).
Original MIT notice: [DATASET-LICENSE](DATASET-LICENSE), copyright 2026 thanu-2928.
The source README names Bhavana Ramancha, Harshith Vinesh Rao Nimmagadda, Geetha Sri
Machineni, Siddi N Shah and Thanmayee Thota. The team took part in building the
combined dataset; this runtime does not reproduce its original multi-source merge
or independently establish every row's institutional lineage.

## Public references and cited research

- ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0 modelled estimates: source and CC BY 4.0 attribution are
  documented in [SoilGrids documentation](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/SoilGrids_faqs.html).
- NASA POWER historical daily meteorology: [NASA POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/).
- Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A archive: [Copernicus Sentinel-2](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/data-collections/copernicus-sentinel-missions/sentinel-2),
  retrieved through [Element 84 Earth Search](https://github.com/Element84/earth-search).
  Per-scene metadata, window files and processing provenance remain bundled.
- Weather providers: [OpenWeather](https://openweathermap.org/current) and
  [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs). Provider terms and API usage limits
  apply; review [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) for hosted/commercial use.
- ICAR / ICAR-CRIDA research and Soil Health Card material are cited individually
  in `backend/data/guidance.json`. The project includes short scoped practice
  summaries, not copied complete articles. Citation does not validate field suitability.

notice is retained in [docs/sources/ARCHIFY-LICENSE](docs/sources/ARCHIFY-LICENSE).

from an external benchmark or actual farmer records. No external project code,
